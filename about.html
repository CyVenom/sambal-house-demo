About this demo

What you are looking at, and how it differs from the original

Sambal House started as a three-person university group project (CIT6224 Web Application Development, Multimedia University): a full e-commerce site for handcrafted chili pastes, written in PHP and MySQL. I was the team leader and the full-stack and security lead. This page is a static rebuild of it so anyone can click through the whole flow without installing XAMPP.

What I built

I wrote the whole application, front to back, as team leader, full-stack developer and security lead.

Accounts and sign-in

  • Registration with email-domain validation, autocomplete and a live password-strength meter
  • Login for customers and a separate admin login
  • Forgot-password and OTP reset, with 15-minute expiry
  • Welcome email on sign-up

Profile and checkout

  • Profile with saved address and Malaysian phone validation
  • Inline change-password with current-password check
  • Email change confirmed by an OTP sent to the new address
  • Checkout with full server-side validation and one-click saved-address prefill

Favourites and reviews

  • Wishlist with a no-reload toggle, a favourites page and a live navbar badge
  • Rate-your-order modal and a read-back of your own reviews
  • Ratings summary with breakdown bars and masked reviewer names on every product page

Admin panel

  • Dashboard with product, order, customer and revenue stats
  • Sales analytics for today, this week and this month
  • Product add, edit, deactivate, reactivate and delete
  • Sales report export to CSV
  • Approve-return and approve-refund actions with 15-second live polling

Security

  • CSRF protection system on every form
  • Prepared statements, output escaping and BCrypt password hashing
  • Session hardening, login rate limiting and one-time payment tokens
  • A written security review: 21 findings from file-upload RCE and payment bypass to CSRF, IDOR and brute force, each fixed and documented

Shop and product pages

  • Catalogue with live search, price, stock and category filters, and sorting
  • Product page with breadcrumb, price box, quantity stepper and image zoom
  • "You may also like" grid on every product
  • All the CSS, hand-written with no framework

Cart, payment and receipts

  • Cart that persists per login, with quantities capped to stock
  • FPX online-banking gateway for 8 Malaysian banks and e-wallets
  • Payment success page and order confirmation email
  • PDF receipt download for paid orders

Orders, tracking and refunds

  • Order history in 7 tabs, with cancel (stock restored) and Pay Now retry
  • 5-step live order tracker with countdown and toast notifications
  • Refund tracker and return tracker, each gated on admin approval
  • Cancellation email, and a live user-activity monitor for admins
  • Admin order detail with customer purchase summary and PDF invoice

Team lead

  • Split the work across three people and kept the codebase and database design consistent
  • Integrated everyone's pages into one working app

Try it

  • Sign in with Try as customer on the login page, or register (the OTP code is shown on screen).
  • Add sambal to the cart, check out, pick a bank, and confirm on the simulated FPX page.
  • Open Track on the order and watch it move from Paid to Shipped to Received to Completed.
  • Once an order is completed you can rate it, and the review appears on the product page.
  • Open the admin panel (one-click demo login) to see the dashboard, change order statuses and edit products.

How the demo works

There is no server and no database. Everything you do is saved in your own browser (localStorage), so your cart, orders and reviews are private to you and disappear if you clear site data. Use Reset demo data in the footer to start over. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
