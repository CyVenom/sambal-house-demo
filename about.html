About this demo

What you are looking at, and how it differs from the original

Sambal House started as a three-person university group project (CIT6224 Web Application Development, Multimedia University): a full e-commerce site for handcrafted chili pastes, written in PHP and MySQL. I was the team leader and the full-stack and security lead. This page is a static rebuild of it so anyone can click through the whole flow without installing XAMPP.

Try it

  • Sign in with Try as customer on the login page, or register (the OTP code is shown on screen).
  • Add sambal to the cart, check out, pick a bank, and confirm on the simulated FPX page.
  • Open Track on the order and watch it move from Paid to Shipped to Received to Completed.
  • Once an order is completed you can rate it, and the review appears on the product page.
  • Open the admin panel (one-click demo login) to see the dashboard, change order statuses and edit products.

How the demo works

There is no server and no database. Everything you do is saved in your own browser (localStorage), so your cart, orders and reviews are private to you and disappear if you clear site data. Use Reset demo data in the footer to start over. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Sample data

The 11 products, prices, stock and descriptions come from the original database. The demo customer and the two older orders under "My Orders" are made-up sample data so the pages aren't empty.
